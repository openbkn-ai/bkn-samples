# Backward_Plan Report

Report one forecast or new customer demand, unique product, quantity, deadline, alternate policy, business date, knowledge network and resolved warehouse scope.

Separate finished stock, allocated finished quantity, net manufacturing quantity and fulfillment mode. Show customer earliest availability and customer delay separately from internal danger/warning/info/unknown evidence. For a full tree, distinguish structural nodes, covered components and net manufacturing nodes.

Include shortages, dated net PO receipts and unresolved arrival/manufacturing facts. Explain any unsupported assumptions without inventing historical causes. With fully specified material supply conditions, report definite confirmed coverage and shortage.

Use the MCP results already collected; perform no new remote queries during report rendering. Suggest human follow-up only; create no monitor, purchase or ERP records.
