# Discoverable Evidence

Knowledge network: `supply_ontology_en`. Discover object types, relations, metrics and named functions through MCP. Use product and material codes from the question and runtime-resolved IDs.

- Product and forecast objects identify the demand, quantity and deadline.
- BOM objects identify hierarchy, usage, main rows and position-specific alternatives.
- Inventory objects provide already-net available quantities and actual warehouse scope.
- Purchase orders provide closure, quantity, net receipts and arrival dates; requests provide traceability only.
- Material objects provide purchase/production lead times.
- Supply_Status and Backward_Plan provide business calculations and row-level evidence.

A count or snapshot metric does not establish historical trends or dated fulfillment. Report the actual function fields and limits; do not invent parameters or fixed expected values.
