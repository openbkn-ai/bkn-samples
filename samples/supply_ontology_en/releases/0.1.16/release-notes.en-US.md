# 0.1.16: kit scheduling and 2027 data

Based on 0.1.15. Adds demand-driven scheduling: a demand forecast is placed on a production line and the result, with the kit check made at that moment, is written to the new kit schedule plan; the source documents are not changed.

**Data**: every date moves six months; the business date is 2027-02-25. Open forecasts roll over 2027-03 to 2027-08, all products of a bill delivered on the same day (the forecast delivery date), with 4 open lines past their delivery date. The documents are reconciled: production orders link to the forecasts they serve, orders in production span the business date and finished ones are all earlier; every open forecast line has a make MRP planned order; purchase orders arrive purchase lead time after they are placed, closed lines on or before the business date, 105 of the 517 open lines overdue; 72 of the 192 owed sales orders are past their promised date; products carry a production lead time by product line. Row counts match 0.1.15.

**New objects**: production lines (plants as parent rows that are never scheduled, with max parallel and product lines), line stoppages (the next three months, two of them plant-wide) and the kit schedule plan (a dataset installed with 13 scheduled and confirmed plans for the coming weeks as scheduling history, two of the confirmed ones not yet in production and relying on open purchases). Products gain a default line code. Six new relations.

**New function and actions**: the kit-schedule function takes the demand of a forecast (whole bill or one product), reserves signed backlog, kit-checks the planned start and writes or updates the plan; an active plan for the same forecast number + product code is updated and its reschedule count grows. It runs three object actions: create (new instance), adjust (modify) and cancel (modify, status Cancelled). Two schedule Gantt rule tools are added, also available as actions for Q&A: Kit_Check reserves signed backlog, then allocates a batch of plans on one shared pool, confirmed before scheduled and each in start order (plans already in production have drawn their materials), flags a start before the earliest kitted date as hard, asks for a purchase request when only unordered materials close the gap, lists the plans competing for the same material, and for one selected plan returns a paged table of its short materials grouped by reason; Line_Can_Build checks that the line's product lines cover every product of the plan. The two calculation actions are unchanged.

The Skills describe the kit schedule plan queries and actions; the 15 experience cards and the v4 reference answers are recomputed on the new data, and 3 questions tied to a date window use the new business date.

**Platform requirements**: the function writes the dataset as the caller and needs the OpenBKN 0.2.2 sandbox deployment (the sandbox injects `BKN_BASE_URL` and `BKN_TOKEN`, and its network policy allows the gateway); uninstalling needs a sample service with openbkn-ai/bkn-samples#260, which removes the sample's datasets first.

Existing installations keep their version; changing versions follows the standard uninstall/install flow, which removes sample-owned data and resources. No in-place migration is provided.
