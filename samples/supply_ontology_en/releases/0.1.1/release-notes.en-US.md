# Supply Chain EN 0.1.1

Prepare the standalone English supply-chain sample for online distribution. Use concise metric names such as **Warehouse Count** and **Total Signed Quantity**, with matching definitions, property bindings, Skills and evaluation references.

The sample includes 12 locked data tables, 14 native functions, 11 metrics and five Skills. Business identifiers, quantities, dates and the deterministic question/answer baseline are unchanged.

This version requires OpenBKN 0.2.x. Installation needs the separately published fixed data image. Online availability requires the package and acceptance metadata to be registered in the official sample catalog. Source merge alone does not make this version installable online.
