# 0.1.12: brings the 产线三维孪生 (3D production line twin) app (OpenBKN 0.2.2)

The data, rule functions, actions and the 机台排产 app are the same as in 0.1.11; objects now show by name.

- **New app 产线三维孪生** (`workshop/apps/line-twin.app.yaml`): one page for a shop floor lead. Figures for machines, running, needing attention, queued operations and open maintenance; a 3D shop floor with the machines placed by plant and work centre, a three-colour light for running, idle and needing attention, crates on a pallet for the queued operations, turned by dragging and zoomed with the wheel; click a machine (or pick it in the list on the right) for its operations and maintenance work orders. All of it is read from the machines, operations and maintenance work orders on the knowledge network and their relations.
- The 3D shop floor is the custom component `x-ai.lineTwin`. Its package is in `workshop/components/ai-lineTwin/`; the package's `index.js` is built, when the installation package is built, from the source and locked dependencies in `workshop/component-sources/ai-lineTwin/`. Installing creates the app, uploads the package into it, writes in the package ID Workshop gives, then publishes it. This needs a sample service that uploads component packages (capability `sample.workshop-components`); a service without it rejects this version before downloading. On a platform without Workshop the sample installs without its apps.
- **Objects show by name**: twelve object types whose names are unique now take the name as their display key, in place of the ID: bills of materials, customers, equipment capabilities, inventory locations, maintenance plans, manufacturing sites, operators, production machines, products, routings, suppliers and work centres. A machine now reads 五轴加工中心 3 号 rather than MACH-MILL-03 in titles, lists and detail panels. The IDs stay the primary keys. Operations, process steps, routing operations, quality specifications, inspection results and shifts keep their IDs, as many of them share a name.
- To upgrade from 0.1.11, uninstall 0.1.11 first. The 50 bkn-eval answers are unchanged.
