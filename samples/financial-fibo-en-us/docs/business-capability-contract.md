# Frozen business and evaluation contract v1

A fictional long-only asset manager needs explainable post-trade control. Users are middle office (settlement breaks), accountant (position/cost/valuation), risk manager (issuer concentration and open sell receivables) and portfolio manager (planned March 2027 impact). Reference scope is equity and dirty-price-per-100 bonds; USD reporting. FIBO anchors semantics, not these operational formulas. Not official NAV, regulatory CCR, investment advice, real market data or trading automation.

P0 decisions: reconstruct dated incremental settlement; distinguish partial, pending, failed, overdue and fully settled; reconstruct trade and settled positions and weighted average remaining cost; assess approved/stale/missing pricing and FX; compute issuer concentration with complete portfolio denominator; compute non-netted unsettled SELL receivables; select approved mandate available at knowledge date and effective at business date; explain breach versus invalid limit selection; compare planned March facts separately from actual facts; trace every conclusion to primary source IDs and flag stale recorded snapshots.

Inputs for each of 3 read-only functions: portfolio_ids (nonempty list of stable portfolio IDs), as_of_date (ISO day), knowledge_date (ISO day), view_kind (actual or planned). settlement_asof reconstructs instructions/transfers; valuation_asof reconstructs quantities, weighted cost, monthly/cumulative realized P&L, valuations and issuer totals; limit_assessment_asof recomputes issuer ratio and sell receivables then selects limits. All share output envelope schema_version=1, capability, scope, completeness (complete/partial/blocked), rows (typed objects), issues (code, affected IDs), evidence (object type to primary-key arrays). Every business row contains relevant stable IDs and status_code; decimal money/quantity/ratio outputs are decimal strings, unknown=null. ISO business dates only. Optional extra diagnostic fields allowed. Unknowns and invalid inputs must not be zero or within-limit. Runtime source loading must detect duplicate IDs, pagination/truncation and errors.

Each settlement row: portfolio_id, trade_id, instruction_id, instrument_id, counterparty_id, side, required_quantity, settled_quantity, remaining_quantity, latest_event_id, status_code, overdue, event_ids. Multiple instructions handled at instruction grain; aggregate transfers by trade when computing positions/receivables. Event quantities are increments; zero acknowledgments add nothing. Consider only event_date<=business date and known_date<=knowledge date, actual only in actual mode; planned mode includes actual plus known plans. Latest status uses event_date,event_sequence; tied conflicting order is ambiguous. Overdue means remaining>0 and due_date<business date, independently of status. Sum cannot exceed required; negative invalid; fully transferred remains settled even later zero acknowledgment. Missing events is pending, not failed.

Position ledger begins with opening quantity and quantity*cost_unit_price*price_multiplier. Eligibility includes trade_date<=business date, known_date<=knowledge date, order_date<=business date and order known_date<=knowledge date; actual excludes planned orders/trades/events. Planned includes actual+known plans. Buys add quantity and unrounded execution cost; sells subtract quantity and weighted average cost, realize quantity*(execution_price*multiplier-average_cost). Monthly realized includes current business month only; cumulative includes all. Long-only oversells invalid. Same-day mixed buy/sell with no reliable source sequence is ambiguous; IDs are not a business ordering rule. Same-side order is immaterial. Settled quantity is opening plus signed eligible incremental transfers. Remaining cost is rounded to cents before FX; market value local rounded to cents then converted/rounded; cost base converted/rounded from booked local cost; unrealized = base MV-base cost. ROUND_HALF_UP. Equities multiplier1; bonds .01. Never mix share/face units.

For valuation select market_prices with as_of_date exactly business date, published_date<=business date, known_date<=knowledge date, price_date<=business date and view matching requested snapshot; actual prices in actual, planned prices in planned future snapshot. Missing, conflicting duplicates, nonpositive, unapproved or older than3 calendar days block valuation. No silent fallback to older approved quote. In actual view publication must be no later than both business and knowledge dates. In planned view, known_date must meet the knowledge cutoff while future published_date is an expected publication date no later than business date; it is a known forecast assumption, never an actual future publication. FX follows the same actual/planned publication rule at the exact snapshot date, from instrument currency to USD, positive; USD/USD=1 without requiring an external identity row; missing/ambiguous non-USD conversion is invalid. Trade-day FX (if requested for turnover) is separately latest actual fixing available on trade day, not snapshot FX. Partial or missing prices make issuer denominator unknown; no priced-only denominator. Issuer ratio=sum issuer USD values/sum all portfolio USD securities values, ratio rounded6 decimal places; zero/negative denominator invalid.

Unsettled receivable = sum over eligible SELL trades of (trade quantity-total eligible transferred quantity)*execution_price*multiplier*snapshot USD FX. No BUY offsets. Sum full-precision contributions then round cents, disclose any difference from separately rounded lines. All trades need complete instruction coverage; missing instruction is unknown, not zero exposure. Booked trade gross is source amount rounded per trade; not raw execution product.

Applicable limit key portfolio/entity/measure; approved, decision_date<=knowledge date, effective_from_date<=business date<=effective_to_date. No eligible version=>unknown, >1=>ambiguous/unknown; threshold>=0, ratios<=1, correct currency (ratio blank, amount USD). Compare exact declared reported precision, observed>threshold breach, equality within; excess=max(0,observed-threshold). Threshold breach is distinct from invalid version selection. No suffix/ID inference. Recorded valuation/exposure/assessment snapshots are diagnostic cached facts only, not oracle; on atomic fact perturbation recalculate and expose stale cache rather than silently using it.


## Component completeness and month-end accounting

Reconstruction retains a known issuer numerator when another issuer's valuation blocks the full portfolio denominator; all corresponding ratios remain null. A proved zero numerator remains zero, while a zero or negative denominator leaves a ratio null. Missing opening positions are unknown. An eligible unresolved trade with a uniquely known issuer blocks only that issuer numerator plus the portfolio denominator; an unlocatable issuer conservatively blocks all issuer numerators. Do not infer component completeness solely from whole-row status.

Recorded `issuer_exposures` can retain null amounts under the original global quality policy. This is a recorded fact, distinct from an independently reconstructed component; never replace one silently with the other. Recorded questions read recorded objects with Cypher, and reconstruction questions use native functions.

For month-end reconciliation group eligible trades by trade month, source local currency and actual/planned view, preserving the knowledge cutoff. Signed booked gross uses each source trade's booked amount (BUY positive, SELL negative); remaining SELL notional uses eligible remaining obligation and execution terms in that local currency. Keep these measures distinct, preserve the original trade cohort across observation dates, and do not carry prior-month trades into a later cohort or assume USD. This reconciliation is a business use of the existing functions and source evidence, not a new function per question.

## Recorded cache reconciliation

After atomic reconstruction, valuation compares recorded positions and valuation snapshots at the same opening record, business date, knowledge cutoff and view. `STALE_RECORDED_POSITION` and `STALE_RECORDED_VALUATION` include cache primary IDs and per-field recorded/recomputed/difference diagnostics; multiple candidates are explicitly ambiguous. Missing recorded snapshots are normal at arbitrary dates. Recorded snapshots never enter the reconstruction. Reliable recomputed values remain available on mismatch; completeness is partial, so report both the values and diagnostics.

Settlement also returns due date, source failure reason and quantity unit; valuation declares base_currency=USD. Unknown market value does not erase reliable reconstructed quantity, local cost or USD cost supported by valid FX. Ratios are dimensionless 0–1 fractions and amounts are USD; recorded exposures are read per dated instance, never accumulated across dates.


## Independently known fields / 独立已知字段

USD/USD is the mathematical identity1. No external FX row is required for USD book cost, USD market value or USD sell receivables. When no identity row exists, fx_id is null and fx_basis=currency_identity (also on receivable contributions); never fabricate a source primary key. A supplied duplicate or invalid USD/USD row is a source conflict: retain its source IDs and report the explicit quality issue, but the mathematical identity remains1 and USD costs/values remain known. Reject its rate and use null fx_id with fx_basis=currency_identity, rather than fabricating an accepted quote. Missing non-USD FX blocks USD conversion, while valid local market value and cost remain available.

Same-day mixed buy/sell without reliable ordering blocks weighted cost and realized/unrealized P&L. It does not erase independently known net trade quantity, signed settled quantity or market value supported by valid price and FX. A genuinely negative long-only day-end position remains invalid. Unknown settlement quantity does not erase reliable trade quantity or cost. Selection must still collect valid quote/FX source IDs when another independent calculation is blocked. Multiple reason_codes may explain a partial row; status_code=blocked does not imply every numerical field is unknown. Cache comparisons use only recomputed fields that are known; an unknown recomputation does not prove a recorded value stale.

A proved zero position has zero local/USD market value without a quote or FX; a proved zero local cost has zero USD cost. A proved zero remaining sell obligation has zero USD receivable without an FX quote. Retain any source-quality diagnostics, but do not erase these mathematical zeros. Such conversions can use null fx_id and fx_basis=zero_amount. Unknown quantity or incomplete instruction coverage never qualifies as proved zero.

## Capabilities and acceptance

Lookups, traces and month-end snapshots use Cypher; the sample uses no metrics. Two read-only Skills serve post-trade operations (middle office, accounting) and risk limits (risk, portfolio manager).
