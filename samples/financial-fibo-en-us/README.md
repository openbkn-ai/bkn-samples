# Financial Portfolio Operations (FIBO reference, EN)

This package is being designed as an end-to-end, fictional investment-portfolio post-trade control sample. Its business requirements are in [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md), its source mapping is in [docs/fibo-operational-mapping.md](docs/fibo-operational-mapping.md), and its BKN contract is in [docs/bkn-modeling-spec.md](docs/bkn-modeling-spec.md).

FIBO is the semantic source, not the sample’s operational dataset. See the [official FIBO repository](https://github.com/edmcouncil/fibo) and the source-and-license section in the mapping document.
