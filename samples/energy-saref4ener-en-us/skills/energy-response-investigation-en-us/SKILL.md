Read the [business capability contract](references/business-capability-contract.md) first. This is read-only synthetic demand-response investigation. Never control devices, execute response commands, pay money, or claim global optimality.

1. Establish event_ids (1..20 distinct IDs), as_of_date and knowledge_date, all dates YYYY-MM-DD. Stop dependent inference and clarify unknown scope; never guess IDs or dates. Discover actual capability IDs with search_capabilities.
2. Route readiness/constraints to flexibility_assessment; deterministic incremental shared-device/site proposals to dispatch_plan; execution receipts, actual measurements, payment threshold and ledger differences to settlement_assessment. Functions resolve the full overlapping event pool, even when only one event is requested.
3. Call at most one native function per run_code. Check isError, exit_code, nonnull result, completeness and issues. Preserve null and evidence for missing/conflicting facts; stop conclusions that depend on unknown values. Snapshots cannot repair failed reconstruction.
4. recorded_eligible_kw reads capacity_snapshots.eligible_kw (kW); recorded_delivery_ratio reads settlement_snapshots.delivery_ratio (dimensionless 1). Each is one recorded event/as-of/knowledge snapshot. Match event and dates exactly and check quality_code. Compare each instance with reconstruction and explain differences; never sum historical snapshots or present them as real-time calculations.
5. kW is average block reduction power; kWh=power×block hours. CNY has two decimal places and quantities/ratios six, half up. Block averages do not prove hourly safety. Future settlement is not_due; cancelled means canceled; unknown is never zero.
6. Classify each issue by its event_ids: requested events versus other events in the full overlapping pool. State affected events, source IDs and business consequences; never attribute another event's declined execution or payment threshold to the requested event. Inspect source offers, commitments or receipts by ID when a causal explanation needs them.
7. For knowledge-cutoff comparisons label primary and reference scope, results, issues and evidence separately, then explain the change. State explicitly when an event is not yet visible. If a reference view exposes it, inspect announced_date and known_date, state the concrete dates and the failed visibility condition; reference facts are not early-view visible evidence. Do not fill it with zero or evidence from the later view. When withdrawal invalidates a prior commitment, explain why the known commitment remains while a new plan is unknown.
8. For end-to-end investigation report readiness, planning, measurement and settlement completeness separately with affected events and blockers. Complete settlement does not establish complete planning; disclose every required partial stage in the end-to-end conclusion. State the signed reconstruction-minus-snapshot difference and unit. Report finish/trace errors separately from business completeness.
9. Preserve the complete evidence from each actual call. Label primary and reference evidence separately; a merged index is supplementary and invented source IDs are prohibited.
10. Explain business facts in English while preserving stable IDs, status_code and issue codes. Cite source IDs, dated scope, missing facts and measured-energy deduplication. Give read-only recommendations; never issue commands, transfer money or make payments.
